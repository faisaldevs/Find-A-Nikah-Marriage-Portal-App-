How the Muzz Marriage tab works

Field notes from driving the live Muzz app on a Pixel 5, focused on the first bottom-nav tab: the one-profile-at-a-time discovery feed. Use it as the reference for our own Marriage tab.

Tested 16 Oct 2026 Signed-in Basic (free) account Member names anonymised

What this is built from

Muzz blocks screenshots and screen recording (Android secure flag), so everything here comes from the accessibility tree: every label, its position and size, and how those change after each tap or scroll. Motion is inferred by sampling positions before and after actions. Colours, imagery and exact animation curves still need the screenshots listed at the end.

The short version

  • One full profile per screen. Not swipe cards. The whole profile is a long vertical page: hero photo, then text sections interleaved with the remaining photos.
  • Actions float. Pass, Compliment and Like sit on the hero photo's bottom edge, then dock above the bottom nav while you read, and rise again at the end of the profile. Undo appears only after a pass.
  • Decisions are instant. Pass or like replaces the profile in under a second and resets the scroll to the top. No match animation unless it is mutual.
  • Gold sells inside the flow. Undo, revealing someone's filters, advanced filters and Sort all open the same Gold paywall with a headline written for that action.
  • Compatibility is a feature. "Check if you match their filters" and "Your similarities" sit right under the hero, before About me.

Profile page order

Top to bottom, as rendered for one member. Photos without text sit between the sections; the count varies by profile (this one had 4).

  1. Hero photo with name, age, distance and chips
  2. Reveal their filters (collapsed card, expands inline)
  3. Your similarities ("You and A already share similarities", e.g. Grew up in Bangladesh)
  4. About me: height in cm and feet, marital status, children
  5. Marriage intentions timeline: Family → Immediately, Chatting → 1–2 months, Marriage → Agree together
  6. Faith: sect, dress, practice, halal food, smoking, alcohol, fasting (emoji chips)
  7. Future plans: wants children, relocation, living with in-laws, polygamy
  8. Photos
  9. Interests (emoji chips, about 13)
  10. Personality (emoji chips incl. MBTI type)
  11. Photos
  12. Education and career

  14. Photo

  16. Verified profile: main photo verified, age verified, ID documents verified

Motion and navigation

  • Floating action row. At rest the buttons straddle the hero photo's bottom edge. As soon as you scroll they settle just above the bottom nav and stay there for the whole profile. At the end of the profile they move up again and sit over the Share / Favourite / Block / Report row.
  • Collapsing top bar. Filters page title and actions move up 12–28px on scroll; the Marriage top bar moves up about 27px.
  • Profile change. Pass and Like replace the content within one sampling interval (under 1 s) and jump back to the top. No card flight was observable from the tree; your recording will show the actual transition.
  • Sheets vs pages. Sort, Boost and the paywall are bottom sheets. Filters, filter detail, Report, Block, Compliment and the gallery are full-screen pages.
  • Paywall reuse. One Gold sheet everywhere (1 week BDT 550, 1 month BDT 850 ≈ 195/week, 3 months ≈ 149/week, "What else do I get with Gold?"), with the headline changed per trigger.

Rule found: one location filter at a time

Distance and Country are mutually exclusive. Switching tabs with one set shows "Remove location filter? Members can only apply one location filter at a time. If you switch to a different location filter, your current one will be removed." with Discard and Cancel. The home country is always included and can only be hidden with Gold.
